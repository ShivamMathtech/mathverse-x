# MASTER PROMPT: MATHVERSE X
## Build an Enterprise-Grade Multidimensional Mathematical Visualization & Computational Studio

### 1. YOUR ROLE

Act as a principal software architect, computational mathematician, numerical-analysis engineer, scientific visualization specialist, graphics programmer, UI/UX designer, and full-stack developer.

Your mission is to design and develop **MATHVERSE X**, a complete mathematical visualization and computational environment inspired by MATLAB, Wolfram Mathematica, GNU Octave, GeoGebra, Desmos, and advanced scientific visualization platforms.

This must be a real, working software product, not a static dashboard, UI prototype, or collection of disconnected demonstrations.

Build a mathematically reliable, extensible, high-performance application that allows users to define, calculate, visualize, transform, analyze, animate, and export mathematical objects across multiple dimensions.

### 2. PRODUCT VISION

The software must support mathematical exploration across:

- 1D: Number lines, scalar functions, sequences and transformations.
- 2D: Cartesian, polar and parametric graphs.
- 3D: Surfaces, solids, curves, vector fields and coordinate systems.
- 4D: Hyperplanes, hypersurfaces, tesseracts and 4D transformations.
- ND: Generalized multidimensional functions, tensors, projections, slices and dimensionality reduction.

The software must support both beginner-friendly graphing and advanced research-level mathematical visualization.
